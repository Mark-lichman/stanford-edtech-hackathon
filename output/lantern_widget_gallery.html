Lantern

A book that starts where a kid already loves something, sends her away from the screen, and ends on purpose.

The screen is a doorway, not a destination. These pieces give one child one clear beat at a time: a page to enter, a margin to clear, a real book to choose, a line to bring home, and a story that is hers because she did something real.
